**CCQs — Concept Checking Questions**  
Questions used to check students actually understand the _meaning_ of new vocabulary or grammar — not just the instructions for an activity, but the language concept itself.

- Teaching "starving" (very hungry): CCQ — "If I'm starving, do I want a small snack or a big meal?" "Have I not eaten for 1 hour or many hours?"
- Teaching present perfect ("I have lived here for 5 years"): CCQ — "Do I still live here now?" "Did I start living here in the past?"

The key with both ICQs and CCQs: never ask yes/no questions like "Understand?" or "Clear?" — always ask something that requires the student to show comprehension.
