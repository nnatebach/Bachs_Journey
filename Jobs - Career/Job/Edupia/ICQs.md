**ICQs — Instruction Checking Questions**  
Quick questions you ask _right after giving instructions_, to confirm students actually understood what to do — before they start the task. You don't ask "Do you understand?" (students will just say yes even if they don't). Instead you ask something that forces them to demonstrate understanding.

- Example instruction: "Work in pairs, write 5 sentences using the past tense."
- ICQ: "Are you working alone or with a partner?" / "How many sentences do you need to write?"
