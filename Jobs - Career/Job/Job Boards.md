**Practical weekly rhythm given your schedule:**

This weighting matches your actual priorities: fastest realistic hire (Tier 1), parallel income while you search (Upwork), and broad coverage without burning limited hours on low-yield boards (Tiers 3-4).

## English Teaching Jobs
Here's the head-to-head for these four, specifically for your teaching job search:

**Quick take:** VietnamWorks and LinkedIn are your highest-value time investment. CareerViet is a solid secondary source. Vieclam24h is worth a passive alert, not active browsing time.

|Platform|What it actually is|Fit for ESL/IELTS teaching|Priority|
|---|---|---|---|
|**VietnamWorks**|Vietnam's oldest and most established professional job portal (Navigos Group, founded 2002); geared toward white-collar/professional roles, strong candidate database that recruiters actively search|Best of the four for education-sector and academic-advisor-type roles; centers with HR departments (VUS, ILA, British Council, corporate training providers) post and search here first|**#1**|
|**LinkedIn**|Global professional network, not Vietnam-specific|Best for remote/online tutoring roles, being discovered by recruiters at larger institutions, and building a visible profile IELTS coordinators or academic directors might search — but smaller local language centers often don't bother posting here|**#1 (tied)**|
|**CareerViet**|Second-tier general job portal, similar audience to VietnamWorks but smaller reach, cheaper for employers to post on|Decent secondary source — you'll see some of the same postings as VietnamWorks plus a few smaller centers that chose it for the lower posting cost|**#2**|
|**Vieclam24h.vn**|Vietnam's highest-_traffic_ job board by volume, but skews toward general/entry-level/local labor market — free posting option means huge volume but lower average job quality/screening|Occasionally has part-time teaching-center postings, but you'll wade through a lot of unrelated listings to find them|**#3 — low active-time priority**|

**Why VietnamWorks and LinkedIn beat the other two for you specifically:** your target employers — established language centers, IELTS-focused academic providers, international schools — are the kind of organizations with real HR teams and budgets, and those are exactly the employers who post on VietnamWorks and actively search LinkedIn. Vieclam24h's free-posting model attracts a much broader, lower-cost employer base (retail, admin, factory, etc.), so your signal-to-noise ratio there is worse.

**How I'd actually spend your time each week:**

- **VietnamWorks (40%)** — keep your profile fully updated, set job alerts for "English Teacher," "IELTS Instructor," "Academic Advisor," and check the search results directly rather than waiting on alerts alone, since employers browse this database.
- **LinkedIn (35%)** — optimize your profile headline/summary around ESL + IELTS + TESOL, follow the language centers you're targeting, and actually message academic directors or HR contacts directly — this often beats applying cold.
- **CareerViet (15%)** — set alerts, check weekly, don't browse daily.
- **Vieclam24h (10%)** — set an alert for your keywords and only check when it fires; not worth scheduled browsing time.

One thing worth layering on top: none of these four are teaching-specific, so pair them with the direct-to-center applications and Facebook teacher groups I mentioned earlier — for language-center hiring in Vietnam specifically, those often move faster than any general job board.
