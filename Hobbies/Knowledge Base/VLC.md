## Video jump sizes
- If you use VLC Media Player, you can customize jump lengths in the settings: [[1](https://www.pie-menu.com/shortcuts/vlc-media-player)]

	- Open VLC and go to **VLC media player** in the top menu bar, then select **Settings** (or press `Command + ,`).
	
	- Click on the **Hotkeys** tab (or search for "jump").
	
	- Look for **Very short jump size**, **Short jump size**, **Medium jump size**, or **Long jump size**.
	
	- Double-click an entry and press your desired key combination to change the shortcut.
	
	- Default short jump shortcuts:
	    - **Very short (3s):** `Command (⌘) + Control (⌃) + Left/Right Arrow`
	    - **Short (10s):** `Command (⌘) + Option (⌥) + Left/Right Arrow`

- Reference: [VLC Media Player shortcuts for Mac](https://www.pie-menu.com/shortcuts/vlc-media-player)
