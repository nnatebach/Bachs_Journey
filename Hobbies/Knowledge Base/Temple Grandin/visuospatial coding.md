Visuospatial coding is ==the brain's mechanism for mapping the spatial arrangement of the visual world into neural activity==, providing a foundational framework that guides general human cognition. [[1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34799253/), [2](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10140574/3/Dekker_TICS%20final.pdf)]

Key Forms

- **Retinotopic maps**: Adjacent cells in the retina project to adjacent neurons in the brain, creating a direct, continuous map of the visual field onto the cortical surface. [[1](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3), [2](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10140574/3/Dekker_TICS%20final.pdf)]

- **Visual field biases**: Coarse-scale positional preferences where certain brain regions respond more strongly to specific areas of the visual field (such as center versus periphery). [[1](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/pdf/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3.pdf), [2](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3)]

Where It Appears in the Brain

- **Visual cortex**: Early and higher-order visual areas that process basic features and complex object categories (like faces or scenes). [[1](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/pdf/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3.pdf), [2](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3)]

- **Beyond visual areas**: Widespread presence across non-visual systems, including the hippocampus (involved in memory and space), the cerebellum (coordination), frontal eye fields (eye movements), and the default mode network (internal thought and rest). [[1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364661321002813), [2](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/pdf/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3.pdf)]

Functional Role

- **Cognitive scaffolding**: Acts as a universal spatial reference frame that helps the brain organize abstract thoughts, memory, and environmental data. [[1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34799253/), [2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364661321002813)]

- **Perception-action loops**: Connects what a person sees directly to how they move and interact with their physical surroundings. [[1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34799253/), [2](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613\(21\)00281-3)]
