# 📚 Core Learning Resources

## Grammar

✅ Grammaire Progressive du Français – Débutant

- This is the same book often sold as **"Avec 400 exercices – Niveau Débutant."**
- Buy **only one**; they're generally the same book with different title formatting.
- Study **2–3 units (lessons) per week**.
- Complete all exercises before moving on.

## Vocabulary

✅ Vocabulaire Progressif du Français – Débutant

Use it alongside the grammar book.

## Flashcards

✅ Anki

Don't rely entirely on premade decks.

Create your own deck from:

- textbook vocabulary,
- useful example sentences,
- verbs,
- common expressions.

Target:

- **15–20 new cards per weekday**
- Review all scheduled cards.

Learning path for Michel Thomas French
### 1. French Foundation (CD1–8)

This is the core course — everything else builds on it. Go through these in strict order; Michel Thomas's method is deliberately cumulative, each CD assumes you've internalized the previous one. Don't skip around.

### 2. French Foundation Review (CD1–2)

Do these right after Foundation, not later. They're consolidation, not new material — the point is to cement what you just built before moving on, since the whole method relies on instant recall of earlier lessons.

### 3. French Builder (CD1–2)

This picks up right where Foundation leaves off, extending sentence complexity and structures. Short course, but treat it as mandatory — it's the bridge into Advanced, not optional bonus content.

### 4. French Advanced (CD1–4)

This is where tenses, more complex structures, and nuance get introduced. It assumes Foundation + Builder are solid — if a CD in here feels like it's moving too fast, that's a signal to loop back to Foundation/Builder rather than push through.

### 5. French Advanced Review (CD1)

Consolidation again, same logic as the Foundation Review — do this immediately after finishing Advanced.

### Where Vocabulary fits

**French Vocabulary (CD1–5)** is the odd one out — it's not sequential/cumulative like the others, it's topic-based vocabulary expansion. Two reasonable ways to use it:

- **Run it in parallel** alongside Foundation/Builder/Advanced, one CD every couple of weeks, as a side track
- Or **save it for after Advanced Review**, as a dedicated vocabulary push once your grammar foundation is fully built

I'd lean toward running it in parallel — vocabulary acquisition benefits from spacing it out rather than cramming it at the end, and it won't interfere with the grammar-logic progression of the main line.

### Full path summary

**Foundation (1–8) → Foundation Review (1–2) → Builder (1–2) → Advanced (1–4) → Advanced Review** — with **Vocabulary (1–5)** threaded in alongside as a parallel track throughout.

Once you finish Advanced Review, you'll be at a strong upper-beginner/lower-intermediate level — that's your cue to shift primary focus toward Coffee Break French, FrenchPod101's Intermediate folder, and start layering in InnerFrench.
