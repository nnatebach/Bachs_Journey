### Most relevant for you
- **Passe-Partout** — the classic 1977–1992 original ran about 6 seasons, roughly 289 episodes. It was revived in 2019 and is still continuing as of early 2026, with 8 seasons and around 349 episodes total across the revival. For your purposes, either era works — the original has more nostalgic cultural-reference value, the revival is more contemporary French. [thetvdb](https://www.thetvdb.com/dereferrer/series/356390)
- **Franklin** — a long-running Canadian series (multiple seasons, well over 100 episodes across the original run plus follow-up series like _Franklin and Friends_) — exact counts vary by which spin-off you count.
- **Cornemuse**, **Bibi et Geneviève** — smaller, less internationally documented shows; episode counts weren’t reliably searchable, likely because they’re more regional/Radio-Canada archive content rather than globally catalogued.
