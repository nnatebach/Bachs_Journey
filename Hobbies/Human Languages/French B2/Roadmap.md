# Learning Philosophy

Instead of asking:

> "What should I finish?"

Ask:

> **"Can I understand it, say it, write it, and recognize it when I hear it?"**

If the answer is **yes**, move on.

## One recommendation

I would make **Assimil the backbone of your daily learning**, with the Progressive books supporting it—not the other way around.

A good mental model is:

- **Assimil** teaches you French in context.
- **Grammaire Progressive** explains _why_ it works.
- **Vocabulaire Progressive** expands your usable vocabulary.
- **Anki** makes what you've learned stick.
- **Pimsleur** turns knowledge into speech.
- **TV5MONDE, RFI, and ChatGPT** bridge the gap between studying and using French in real life.

Following this roadmap consistently should put you in a strong position to reach B2 and prepare effectively for the French language requirements associated with Canadian immigration.
