To reach **B2 French** for Canadian immigration, you'll be developing much more than just vocabulary and grammar. Here's the complete set of skills, in the order I'd prioritize them.

|Skill|Why it matters|Main Resources|
|---|---|---|
|**Listening Comprehension**|Understand spoken French in conversations, media, and exams|Assimil, Pimsleur, TV5MONDE, RFI, Coffee Break French|
|**Speaking (Conversation)**|Communicate naturally and respond without translating|Pimsleur, ChatGPT|
|**Pronunciation**|Be understood clearly and improve listening|Pimsleur, Assimil (shadowing), ChatGPT|
|**Vocabulary**|Build the words needed for everyday and professional communication|Vocabulaire Progressive, Anki, Assimil|
|**Grammar**|Form correct sentences and improve writing/speaking accuracy|Grammaire Progressive|
|**Reading**|Understand articles, signs, emails, and exam texts|Assimil, TV5MONDE, graded readers|
|**Writing**|Write messages, emails, opinions, and exam responses|ChatGPT, Grammaire Progressive exercises|
|**Active Recall**|Retain vocabulary and grammar long term|Anki|
|**Automaticity (Thinking in French)**|Reduce translating from your native language|Pimsleur, ChatGPT, self-talk|
|**Cultural Competence**|Understand French-speaking customs and expressions|Coffee Break French, TV5MONDE, RFI|

## Skills by Phase

### Phase 1 (Months 1–3): Foundation

Focus on:

- ✅ Pronunciation
- ✅ Listening
- ✅ Basic speaking
- ✅ Core vocabulary
- ✅ Present tense grammar
- ✅ Reading simple sentences
- ✅ Writing simple sentences

---

### Phase 2 (Months 4–6): Everyday Communication

Add:

- ✅ Everyday conversations
- ✅ Past and future tenses
- ✅ Reading short texts
- ✅ Writing short paragraphs
- ✅ Faster listening

---

### Phase 3 (Months 7–12): Intermediate

Develop:

- ✅ Longer conversations
- ✅ Listening to authentic French
- ✅ Reading news and stories
- ✅ Expressing opinions
- ✅ Intermediate grammar

---

### Phase 4 (Months 13–18): B2

Master:

- ✅ Debates
- ✅ Formal writing
- ✅ Native-speed listening
- ✅ Fluency
- ✅ Complex grammar
- ✅ Professional vocabulary

## The Four Core Language Skills

Everything ultimately supports these four abilities:

1. 🎧 **Listening** – understanding spoken French.
2. 🗣️ **Speaking** – expressing yourself clearly and naturally.
3. 📖 **Reading** – understanding written French.
4. ✍️ **Writing** – communicating accurately in writing.

Around these, you'll build supporting skills such as pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary, and memory.

### If I were to simplify your entire journey into one sentence:

> **Listen → Understand → Speak → Read → Write → Review → Repeat.**

Keeping those six actions in balance is what will steadily move you from complete beginner to the B2 level you're aiming for.
