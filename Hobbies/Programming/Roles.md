# My verdict

If you complete the Frontend Masters curriculum we've discussed and supplement it with:

- **UX/UI design** (Figma, design systems, accessibility)
- **PostgreSQL** (deeper database knowledge)
- **Docker**
- **One cloud platform** (AWS is a good default)
- **CI/CD** (GitHub Actions, deployment pipelines)

you'll cover **well over 90%** of what appears across the roadmap.sh paths you've listed **that is relevant to your goals**.

The remaining items on roadmap.sh (such as C++, advanced distributed systems, ML research, or framework-specific ecosystems you don't intend to use) are valuable for other specializations, but they aren't necessary to become a strong full-stack engineer who can design, build, deploy, and maintain modern web, mobile, and AI-powered applications.
