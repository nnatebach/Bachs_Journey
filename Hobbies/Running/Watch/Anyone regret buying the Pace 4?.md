Yes, there are absolutely buyers who regret purchasing the **COROS Pace 4**. While it received strong reviews overall, specific community discussions across Reddit (`r/Coros`) reveal a clear pattern among those returning the watch or wishing they had gone with another brand (like Garmin or Apple).

The primary reasons for buyer's remorse generally fall into four camps:

### 1. The Gimmicky "Action Button" Lockdown

One of the most vocal complaints stems from how COROS handled the newly added third physical button.

- **The Regret:** COROS integrated a beta Voice Control assistant and mapped it directly to a long-press of the Action Button. Many users feel this is an unpolished gimmick and are deeply frustrated because **you cannot rebind or customize this button shortcut** to standard, practical functions like a quick stopwatch, timer, or alarm. If you don't care for voice controls, it effectively renders a physical piece of hardware useless.
    

### 2. Upgrading from Apple Watch or Garmin for "Health & Wellness"

Many fitness beginners or lifestyle users bought the Pace 4 expecting a holistic health tracker, only to find it hyper-focused on performance data.

- **The Regret:** Users coming from an Apple Watch or feature-dense Garmins frequently complain that the on-watch UI feels sparse or "cheap." They find themselves digging into the phone app just to look at sleep patterns, HRV, or daily steps. For people who want automated lifestyle coaching (like Garmin's Body Battery or Apple's seamless smart features), the Pace 4 can feel like a rigid, stripped-down spreadsheet.
    

### 3. Early Batch GPS & Elevation Bugs

While COROS is widely praised for incredibly fast and helpful customer service, early batches of the Pace 4 suffered from notable hardware instability.

- **The Regret:** Dozens of threads note units where GPS signals would randomly drop mid-run, and kilometer/mile splits wouldn't align with reality. A common theme is jagged, "saw-toothed" elevation tracking that overestimates climbs. Even though COROS routinely issues hassle-free warranty replacements for these defective units, early adopters were highly frustrated by having their structured training data messed up right out of the box.
    

### 4. Poor Charging Cable Connection

- **The Regret:** COROS stuck with their traditional plug-in charging port rather than moving to a more modern magnetic puck. Long-term users are vocal about how easily the cable disconnects if the watch is bumped while sitting on a desk, sometimes waking up to a dead watch right before a morning workout.
    

### The Consensus on Regret

If someone bought the Pace 4 purely as a **lightweight, no-nonsense tool to track runs, paces, and structural workouts**, they rarely regret it. The regret is almost exclusively concentrated among people who wanted a seamless smart/wellness companion, or early adopters who got bit by the first-wave manufacturing defects.
