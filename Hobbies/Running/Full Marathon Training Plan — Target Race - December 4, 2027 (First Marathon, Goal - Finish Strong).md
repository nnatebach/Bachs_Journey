## Standing guidelines throughout

- **Easy pace = conversational pace.** Most of your weekly volume (roughly 80%) should be at this effort, not the 6:00–7:00/km you've been running for "easy" runs recently.
- **Increase weekly mileage by no more than ~10%**, with a step-back week every 3–4 weeks.
- **Sunday swim stays in the routine** — it supports recovery without adding impact stress on your legs.
- **Strength training is optional, not required.** You're not doing it currently, and the plan works fine without it. If you want to add it later, 1–2x/week focused on glutes, hips, and calves would help running durability — but it's a nice-to-have, not something to feel behind on.
- **Shoe rotation**: alternate two pairs of trainers to reduce repetitive loading; save your lighter shoes for tempo/race days.
- **Fuel long runs over 90 minutes**: practice race-day nutrition (carb intake ~30-60g/hour) on your Phase 3 long runs, never for the first time on race day.
- **Listen to sharp or one-sided pain** — dull general fatigue is normal marathon training; a specific, localized pain that changes your gait is a signal to back off and, if it persists, see a physio.
