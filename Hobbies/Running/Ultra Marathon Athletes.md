## Adventure and ultra-distance runners

- William Goodge – Runs multi-day, cross-country, and charity endurance challenges. Shares plenty of training and behind-the-scenes content.
- Russ Cook – Documented his run across Africa. His videos focus on perseverance, logistics, and the realities of long-distance expeditions.
- Tom Evans – Elite, but his content is practical and often centered on training and racing.
- Lucy Gossage – Balances medicine with high-level ultrarunning and shares thoughtful insights.
- John Kelly – Famous inside the ultra community for tackling incredibly difficult races while maintaining a regular career.

## YouTubers who live the lifestyle

- Jeff Pelletier – One of the best channels for beautiful race documentaries, training, and adventure runs.
- Kofuzi – Primarily road and marathon running, but also covers ultramarathons, gear, and race experiences.
- Ben Parkes – Marathon-focused with occasional ultras and excellent educational content.
- Ryan Clayton – Honest training logs, racing, and trail adventures.
- Seth James DeMoor – Covers everything from daily training to mountain ultras.

## Trail and mountain specialists

- Kilian Jornet – Extremely accomplished but still deeply connected to mountain exploration rather than celebrity culture.
- Courtney Dauwalter – Legendary within ultras, yet known for being humble and authentic.
- Joe McConaughy – FKTs, unsupported adventures, and long wilderness efforts.
- Damian Hall – Trail running, environmental advocacy, and approachable content.
- Sally McRae – Shares the emotional and mental side of endurance sports.

## Lesser-known but inspiring

- Quek Shio – Documents ambitious endurance projects and consistent training.
- Mark Lewis – Funny, relatable, and takes on increasingly difficult endurance events.
- Martin Johnson – Training, races, and the realities of balancing life with endurance sports.
- Simon Wilson – Long-distance challenges with an emphasis on exploration.
